## IwaraTV 2.1.1

### Fixed

- **IwaraTV now quits when you close its window.** While you were signed in, it kept running in the background for up to 5 minutes after closing. During that time, starting IwaraTV again (installed or portable) showed no window, so the app looked broken.

### Updating

- **From 2.1.0:** IwaraTV offers this update itself. The Windows installer and AppImage download it in the background; choose **Restart to update**. Portable, ZIP, `.deb` and macOS copies download it when you click **Download**.
- **From 2.0 or earlier:** install 2.1.1 by hand once (see the [2.1.0 notes](https://github.com/demogest/IwaraTV/releases/tag/v2.1.0)). Later versions then arrive inside the app.

### 中文摘要

- **修复**：登录状态下关闭窗口后，IwaraTV 会在后台继续运行最多 5 分钟；在此期间再次启动 IwaraTV（安装版或便携版）不会显示窗口。现在关闭窗口即退出应用。
- **更新方式**：2.1.0 会在应用内提示此更新（Windows 安装版和 AppImage 在后台下载，点击**重启以更新**即可）；2.0 及更早版本请手动安装一次 2.1.1。
